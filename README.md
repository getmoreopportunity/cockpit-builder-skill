# Cockpit Builder (free)

**Stop explaining your entire business to AI. Every. Single. Time.**

Cockpit Builder is a free skill your own AI runs. It interviews you with 10 plain-language questions, shows you a map of what you run and sell, then writes three small files your AI can read every time you start work:

| File | What's in it |
|---|---|
| `LLM.md` | You, your businesses, your offers, who buys, the tools you use, what stays yours |
| `BRAIN.md` | Your current priority, active work, open questions and a dated log |
| `START-HERE.md` | How to hand these files to any AI and get to work |

Then it helps you finish one real task tied to your priority.

## Use it in 2 minutes
Copy the prompt in **[PROMPT.md](PROMPT.md)** into ChatGPT, Claude, Grok or Gemini. Full steps: **[START-HERE.md](START-HERE.md)**.

The skill itself lives in [`fpf-cockpit-builder/SKILL.md`](fpf-cockpit-builder/SKILL.md).
